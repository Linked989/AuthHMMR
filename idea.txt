Here is how is decided if devices are authenticated:
The gateway will collect all the votes and calculate the final weights resulting from the weight-based consensus algorithm. Following this process, an IoT device is considered authenticated if the weight of the YES vote is equal to or greater than 50% of the total weight of all IoT devices that voted:
Wyes >= 0.5 x Wtotal
